Open the exported `.docx` in Word. Each diagram is made of real Word objects:

- **SmartArt** (a process, a hierarchy, a cycle): click it, then **SmartArt Design → Add Shape** to add a
  step or a branch; change its colours or style from the same tab.
- **Word chart** (`pie`): **Chart Design → Edit Data** changes the figures.
- **Shapes** (every other diagram): click a box to retype it, drag it — the arrows stay attached.

The people you send the document to can do all of this too, without Markdown or Mermaid.
