At the top of every Markdown file, one line of links for the whole document:

- **Export to Word** — converts the whole document (text, tables, every diagram) to `.docx`.
- **Export to PowerPoint** — one slide per diagram, titled with the heading above it (shown when the
  document has at least one diagram).
- **Settings** — page layout, fonts, diagrams, output folder.

Above each mermaid block, **Export this diagram…** exports that diagram alone, to Word or to a
one-slide PowerPoint deck.

Other ways to trigger the same export: the status bar item at the bottom, right-click the file in the
Explorer, the editor or its tab (**md2nativedocx** menu), or the Command Palette
(`Ctrl+Shift+P` → "md2nativedocx").
