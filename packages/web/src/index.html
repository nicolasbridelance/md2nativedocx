Markdown to Word, diagrams included

Paste Markdown with Mermaid or PlantUML diagrams, get a Word document where every diagram is real, editable shapes, SmartArt or charts. Not pictures.

  • Every box is a Word shape you can retype and move
  • SmartArt and Word charts with editable data
  • Everything happens in your browser: your text is never sent anywhere.

      How it works

      1. Write or paste Markdown

        Headings, tables, lists, code, maths, and diagrams in ```mermaid or ```plantuml blocks. Or open a .md file.

      2. Converted in your browser

        Pandoc, the standard document converter, runs inside this page. The diagrams are drawn as native Word objects, not as pictures.

      3. Edit everything in Word

        Click a box to retype it, drag it and its arrows follow, restyle a SmartArt, change a chart’s figures with Edit Data.
